# Privacy Policy for Scripture Memory

**Last Updated:** July 24, 2026

**Scripture Memory** ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data of our users ("you" or "user"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and related services (collectively, the "Application").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access or use the Application.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Data Provided by You
When you create an account or sign in to the Application (e.g., via Google Sign-In or Firebase Authentication), we may collect:
- **Account Information:** Your name, email address, user ID, and profile image provided by your authentication provider.

### B. App Activity and User Content
To deliver the core scripture memorization functionality, we collect and store:
- **Verse Progress & Practice Data:** Scripture verses you save, your practice history, recitation scores, and review schedules.
- **App Settings & Preferences:** User interface preferences and app configuration settings.

### C. Device Permissions and Sensor Data
The Application requests specific permissions on your mobile device to enable interactive features:
- **Microphone & Audio Access (`RECORD_AUDIO`):** Used strictly during spoken recitation practice to capture audio for speech recognition.
- **Speech Recognition:** Used to convert your spoken recitation into text to grade and verify your accuracy against the selected scripture verse.

> **Note on Audio Data:** Audio captured during recitation practice is processed for real-time speech-to-text recognition and is not retained or stored permanently on our servers after recognition completes.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
- Create, manage, and secure your user account.
- Synchronize your scripture memory progress across devices using cloud storage.
- Provide real-time feedback on spoken recitation practice via speech recognition.
- Save your app preferences and settings locally and in the cloud.
- Maintain and improve the performance, reliability, and security of the Application.

---

## 3. Storage and Data Retention

- **Cloud Storage:** Your progress data and account information are securely stored in **Google Firebase (Firestore & Authentication)**.
- **Local Storage:** Certain settings, cached verses, and offline progress are stored locally on your device using local storage (`AsyncStorage`).
- **Data Retention:** We retain your account and progress data for as long as your account remains active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.
